Questions

Frequently asked

Do you make furniture to our own drawings?

Yes. Send drawings, dimensions or a reference image. We return a detailed drawing, material board and quotation before production.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Most pieces start at one unit. MOQ is shown on every product card; project quantities are quoted individually.

Can you match a specific finish or material?

Yes. Wood, veneer, leather, fabric, brass, stainless steel and glass can be matched to a supplied sample. We confirm with a material board first.

How long does production take?

Typical lead time is 25–35 days after drawing and sample approval. Lead time for each piece is listed in its specification.

Do you handle export packing and shipping?

Yes. Export-grade wooden crating and protection are available, and we can arrange shipment to your port or consolidate with your forwarder.

Can we visit the factory or inspect goods before shipment?

Yes. Factory visits and third-party inspection can be arranged. Tell us your schedule and we will coordinate.

Which certificates can you provide?

CE, FCC, RoHS, EAC, E0 panel and CA117 documentation. See our certificates page, or request the scan set with your quotation.

How do we get a quotation?

Use Request a Quote or WhatsApp with the product code, quantity, destination and target date. We reply with a written specification and price.
