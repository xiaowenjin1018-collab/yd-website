Before you enquire

Joinery FAQ

What do you need to quote a joinery project?

Elevations / floor plans, dimensions, finish requirements and project location. The more complete the information, the more accurate the itemised quote. A reference image is enough for an initial estimate.

Can you supply wall panelling only, without cabinetry?

Yes. Each of the four systems can be commissioned separately or delivered as a complete package. Same-batch production keeps grain and colour consistent.

Who handles site installation?

Components ship numbered with installation drawings. We can arrange technicians on site to guide assembly and finishing; details are confirmed in the quotation.

Send us your drawings

We reply within one business day with design advice, an itemised quote and the production schedule.
