12 / Questions

Before you enquire

What is the minimum order quantity?

Most pieces are made to order with an MOQ of one. Project pricing applies to larger quantities. Tell us your quantity and we will quote accordingly.

Can you customise sizes, materials and colours?

Yes. Dimensions, upholstery, finishes and metalwork are all configurable. Send a drawing or reference image and our team will return a detailed specification.

What is the typical lead time?

Standard finishes are usually 25–35 days after drawing approval. Bespoke material development may take longer; we confirm the exact schedule in the quotation.

Do you handle export shipping and documentation?

We pack for international transit and coordinate shipping to your port or warehouse. Export documents and material declarations can be provided on request.

How do I get a quotation?

Use the Request a Quote form with your specification, quantity and timeline. Our team replies within one business day with options, pricing and lead time.
